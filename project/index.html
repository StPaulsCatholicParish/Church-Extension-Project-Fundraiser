Proposed St. Paul Church Extension

Designed by M2ERE Design Partners, the proposed extension will expand Saint Paul's total worship area to 456 m² — nearly doubling our current capacity. The single-storey addition adjoins the existing structure along the southern elevation, introducing a generous new nave with pew seating, louvred windows for natural ventilation, and a sheltered entry porch. The design preserves the character of our existing church while giving Gerehu's growing Catholic community the dignified, permanent home it deserves.
