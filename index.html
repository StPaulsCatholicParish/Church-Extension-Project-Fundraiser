About Our Parish

For many years, Saint Paul's Catholic Parish – Gerehu Stage 6 has stood as a beacon of hope, faith, and positive change within our community. The parish has opened its doors to countless young people who felt lost, forgotten, or without direction.

Through community service, G4G security initiatives, sports programs, youth activities, and faith-based guidance, the church has helped guide many away from petty crime and towards purpose, discipline, and hope for a brighter future.

The Fundraising Dinner

Building a Church, Building a Future for Our People.

As our congregation continues to grow, we are now fundraising to build an extension for our church — not just a bigger building, but a stronger future for our people and community.

All proceeds from this event will go directly towards the Saint Paul's Parish Church Building Extension Project. Every contribution helps us build a place where faith, hope, unity, and community can grow for generations to come.

Proposed Extension

The Building We Are Raising Together

The proposed church extension — designed by m2ere Design Partners — will expand Saint Paul's worship area to 455 m², giving our growing congregation the space it deserves. The new wing seamlessly adjoins the existing structure, preserving the spirit of the original church while creating a light-filled, modern gathering space for Gerehu for generations to come.
